# Office Core surface textures

CC0 assets from ambientCG, reduced to 256 × 256 JPEG (quality 72), with neutralised colour and softened contrast. Only base colour maps are shipped; textures and materials are shared. No normal/displacement maps or external runtime requests.

- wood.jpg: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood049
- carpet.jpg: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet011
- plaster.jpg: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PaintedPlaster006
- License: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

The aged plaster, painted panel and concrete variants are procedural edits of plaster.jpg. carpet-worn.jpg adds procedural dirt and scuffs to carpet.jpg. These variants retain the same CC0 source attribution.
